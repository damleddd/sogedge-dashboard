Methodology & limitations

Projection model

Baseline estimated shots = 45% last-10 shots per game + 20% last-5 shots per game + 35% current-season shots per game. Opponent adjustment uses the opposing team's average shots against relative to the NHL baseline of 29.5, with the adjustment capped at ±15%. The auto scanner uses the same baseline and opponent adjustments. It excludes future games from the sample when scanning historical dates. The model is deliberately simple and has not been backtested or calibrated.

Shot counts are modeled using a Poisson distribution to estimate over/under probabilities. This is a rough approximation; NHL player shot distributions can be overdispersed. Fair odds, implied probabilities, and expected ROI are estimates, not guarantees.

Data & odds

Game schedules, rosters, player logs and standings are requested live from the public NHL API. Some endpoints may change or be unavailable, and the NHL may block cross-origin requests from certain hosting environments. Opponent shots against is taken from current-season standings if provided. If unavailable, the app clearly marks the matchup adjustment as neutral.

FanDuel odds are manual inputs. The app does not scrape FanDuel, place wagers, access an account, or guarantee that entered odds remain available. It does not automatically incorporate injuries, projected lines, opponent position-specific suppression, or confirmed goalie assignments.

Responsible use

Recent hit rates are descriptive, not calibrated probabilities. Check scratches, line changes, and odds before wagering. Use only where legally permitted, and never treat the rankings as certain outcomes.

Independent research tool. Not affiliated with NHL or FanDuel. No wagering functionality. All times are displayed in your device timezone.
