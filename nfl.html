NFL EDGE / tools

Probability calculator

Estimate the probability of going over or under a prop line using your own projected average and standard deviation.

How it works

Normal distribution estimate

Uses a normal distribution with your input mean and standard deviation to estimate over/under probabilities.

The example inputs are illustrative, not actual player projections. Yardage and receptions may not follow a normal distribution, and the output does not account for injuries, game scripts or model uncertainty.

Odds comparison uses break-even probability; it does not account for changes in the line or market limits.
